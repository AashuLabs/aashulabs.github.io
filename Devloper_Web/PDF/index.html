PDF - Privacy Policy

App: PDF

Developer: Aashu_Labs

Package: com.pdf.manager

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Contact: ashulabs.support@gmail.com

1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application PDF (package com.pdf.manager).

2. Overview

PDF is a document and PDF utility that provides PDF viewing, PDF creation, merging, splitting, compression, conversion, document scanning, OCR, editing and organizing pages, signing, watermarking, document conversion, and related tools. Most document processing is performed locally on your device. Some features use network-connected platform or third-party services as described below.

3. Files and Documents

The app accesses files and documents that you select or create to perform the operation you request. Document contents are processed locally unless you intentionally use a network-related feature such as URL to PDF or interact with an external service. Output files may be saved to locations you choose or to app-created folders such as Documents/PDF and Pictures/PDF. The developer does not operate a document-upload server for this app.

4. Camera

Camera permission is used for Scan to PDF and document-scanning functionality. Camera access is used only when you invoke a scanning feature. The app does not request permanent or background camera access.

5. Storage / Files

The app uses Android file and document APIs, including MediaStore and the Storage Access Framework where applicable, to open, create, edit, share, and save documents. Legacy Android storage permissions apply only on older Android versions where required by the platform.

6. OCR and Document Scanning

OCR and document scanning use Google ML Kit and Google Play services components. Document and OCR input and results are processed on-device by these components and are not uploaded to the app developer. Google Play services may download or update required machine-learning components. Google ML Kit may send device and app information, identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, and event or error codes to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. These technical metrics do not include your document images or recognized text. Google's applicable privacy terms also apply.

7. URL to PDF / Internet Access

The app includes a URL-to-PDF feature. When you manually enter a website URL and request conversion, Android WebView connects to that website to load its content. The website may receive normal network information such as your IP address, browser or WebView request information, and cookies depending on the site. The third-party website's own privacy policy applies to that interaction. The developer does not operate a proxy server for this feature.

8. Local App Data

Settings, preferences, recent-file or operation-history information, and app state may be stored locally using Android local storage, Room, and DataStore as used by the application. The app does not provide cloud sync and currently has no developer-operated account or login service.

9. Android Backup

The app allows Android backup. Settings and some app-internal data may be included in Android backup or device transfer when enabled by you or your device. App-private signatures, converted document copies, and legacy PDF files are excluded by this app's backup rules. Public documents in Documents/PDF or Pictures/PDF are outside app-private backup and may be handled separately by your device or backup provider. The app developer does not personally receive Android backups.

10. Google Play In-App Review

The app uses Google Play's in-app review functionality. Local usage information may be used on the device to decide when to offer a review prompt. Interaction with Google Play is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

11. Support and Feedback

When you voluntarily choose Feature Request or Bug Report, the app opens an email flow addressed to ashulabs.support@gmail.com. The prepared email may contain text you enter, the device manufacturer and model, Android version, and app version. The email is sent only after your action through your email application; it is not automatically uploaded by the app.

12. Advertising and Analytics

The app does not currently include advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or developer-operated analytics tracking. Google ML Kit and Play services may collect technical diagnostics and usage metrics as described in section 6. Local app usage state used for functionality, such as in-app review eligibility, is not sent to a developer-operated analytics service.

13. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell user documents or personal information. Information may be handled by external services only when needed for the relevant user-requested function, such as loading a website, using Google Play services, sending an email, or sharing a document with an app you select.

14. Data Retention

Documents created by the app remain on your device or chosen storage until you delete them. Cached and temporary files may be removed by the app. Local app data can generally be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Emails voluntarily sent to support may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and maintain support records.

15. Data Deletion

Because the app has no user account service, there is no online account database that requires an account-deletion system. You can delete generated documents yourself and clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data. For privacy requests relating to support email, contact ashulabs.support@gmail.com.

16. Security

The app uses reasonable Android platform security practices, including scoped or user-selected file access and FileProvider for secure sharing where applicable. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

17. Third-Party Services

Depending on the feature you use, the app interacts with Google Play services, Google ML Kit, Google Play In-App Review, Android WebView, and third-party websites that you select. Those services are governed by their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, service usage, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

19. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact ashulabs.support@gmail.com.
