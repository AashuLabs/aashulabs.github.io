Royal Parking – Privacy Policy

App: Royal Parking

Developer: Aashu_Labs

Platform: Android / Google Play

Effective Date: 18 October 2026

Contact: ashulabs.support@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Aashu_Labs handles information in connection with Royal Parking, an Android car-parking game. It covers gameplay data, support communications, Google Play distribution, relevant third-party services, retention, and privacy choices. It applies to Royal Parking only.

2. Game Progress and Settings

Royal Parking saves completed and unlocked levels and your sound, music, and vibration preferences locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. These saves help retain your progress and settings between sessions. The reviewed game does not use an Aashu_Labs account or cloud-save service for this information.

Clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it may remove these saves. Android backup or device transfer, if enabled for your device and app, may affect whether some data is restored.

3. Accounts and Support Communications

The reviewed game does not require you to create an account with Aashu_Labs. If you email ashulabs.support@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and provide support. Please avoid sending passwords or payment-card details.

4. Device Data and Permissions

The reviewed Android release manifest requests internet access. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permission. Its additional app-specific permission is restricted to the app's own components. Internet access alone does not establish that gameplay data is sent to Aashu_Labs.

Google Play and your device may process information independently when you install, update, or use the game, subject to their own privacy practices.

5. Advertising

No advertising SDK or in-game advertising integration was found in the reviewed Royal Parking project. This statement describes the reviewed build; advertising and any related data practices must be reassessed if the release build changes.

6. Google Play Purchases and Distribution

If you buy or download Royal Parking through Google Play, Google handles the store transaction, payment details, downloads, and related account information under its own terms and privacy policy. The reviewed game has no in-game purchase flow and does not ask you to enter payment-card details within the game.

7. Third-Party Services

Royal Parking is built with the Unity game engine and is intended for distribution through Google Play. The reviewed Unity project has Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, Unity Cloud Diagnostics, and Unity Purchasing disabled. The presence of the Unity engine does not by itself mean that these optional services are active.

See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's practices and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for Unity's applicable practices.

8. How Information Is Used and Shared

Local progress and preferences are used to maintain the game experience. We use information you voluntarily send by email to answer your request. Google Play and any applicable platform services process information under their own policies. We may disclose support information when required by law or to protect users, our rights, or security, where permitted by law.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved progress and preferences generally remain on your device until they are changed, the game's app storage is cleared, or the game is uninstalled. Android backup or device transfer may retain or restore a copy under the applicable device settings.

Support emails may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations. For questions about an email you sent or a deletion request, contact ashulabs.support@gmail.com. Data handled independently by Google or another provider is subject to that provider's controls and retention practices.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can change sound, music, and vibration preferences in the game's settings. Android settings can be used to manage available device-level controls and clear the game's app data. You may contact us about support correspondence or other applicable privacy requests. Choices for information processed by Google or another provider depend on that provider's controls and applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Aashu_Labs handles children's information in accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play requirements. Parents or guardians with questions about Royal Parking's privacy practices may contact us using the address below.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to safeguard information we handle, including support communications. Device, store, and other third-party providers apply their own security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. International Processing

Google Play, your email provider, and any other services you use may process information in countries other than your own under their respective privacy policies and applicable protections.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when Royal Parking, its SDKs, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The Effective Date above will be updated when appropriate.

15. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Royal Parking, contact Aashu_Labs at ashulabs.support@gmail.com.
