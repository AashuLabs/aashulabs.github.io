Bottle Colours - Privacy Policy

App: Bottle Colours

Developer: Aashu_Labs

Package: com.bottlecolours.game

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Contact: ashulabs.support@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains the information practices of Aashu_Labs for Bottle Colours, an Android Water Sort puzzle game. It covers locally stored game data and preferences, support communications, advertising and third-party services, and privacy and consent choices.

2. Game Progress and Local Data

Bottle Colours stores certain gameplay information and preferences locally on your device to preserve your game experience. This may include:

  • Current level, highest or unlocked level progress, and other game progress
  • Coin balance or related game state, where applicable
  • Sound, music, vibration, and language preferences
  • Other local gameplay or settings state

Bottle Colours does not provide an Aashu_Labs cloud sync service for this information. Gameplay progress and settings stored by the game's normal local save system remain on your device and are not transmitted to Aashu_Labs as part of normal gameplay.

This describes the game's own local save system. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process other information separately, as explained below.

3. User Accounts

Bottle Colours does not require you to create an account or register with Aashu_Labs.

4. Personal Information

Bottle Colours does not directly require you to provide your name, phone number, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data, or precise GPS location to play the game. The game does not request precise GPS location.

If you choose Contact Support, the game may open your email app so you can send a message to ashulabs.support@gmail.com. You decide whether to send an email and what information to include. Information in a support email is provided voluntarily by you.

5. Advertising

Bottle Colours uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process and send information to Google, including IP address, device identifiers, advertising or account identifiers where available or applicable, app and ad interactions, user or product interaction information, and diagnostic or performance information. Google may use this information to deliver advertisements, measure ad performance, provide advertising-related analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain security.

Google may use an IP address to estimate a device's approximate or general location. Bottle Colours itself does not request precise GPS location for advertising.

The type of advertising and use of data may depend on your privacy choices, consent status, region, device or platform settings, and Google's applicable advertising configuration. Depending on these factors, users may receive personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise privacy-restricted advertising.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Bottle Colours uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to manage advertising privacy and consent choices. Consent or privacy information may be requested when the game starts, and eligible users may be shown a Google privacy or consent form. Available choices depend on region and applicable legal requirements.

Advertising requests use the applicable consent and privacy status. The game does not require every user to make the same choice or receive the same type of ad.

7. Third-Party Services

Bottle Colours uses the following third-party services for distribution, advertising, and consent handling:

  • Google Play
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Google processes information under its own privacy practices. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising and privacy information.

8. Children's Privacy

Bottle Colours is intended for a general audience. Aashu_Labs configures the game, advertising services, and privacy and consent handling in accordance with applicable Google Play policies, age-related requirements, and privacy laws. Where a user's region or applicable requirements call for additional consent or advertising restrictions, the relevant platform and advertising configuration is used to respect them.

9. Data Retention

Local game data: Gameplay and settings data generally remains on your device until you clear the game's app storage or data, uninstall it, or reset the data through available device or app functionality.

Support email: Support communications may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide support, and maintain appropriate support records.

Third-party services: Google and other independent third-party services may apply their own retention practices. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's practices.

10. Data Deletion

Bottle Colours does not require an Aashu_Labs account, so there is no Aashu_Labs cloud account containing your game progress. Local gameplay and settings data can generally be removed by clearing Bottle Colours app storage or data, or by uninstalling the game.

For questions or requests about information you voluntarily provided in a support email, contact ashulabs.support@gmail.com. Information independently controlled by Google or another third-party service may need to be managed through that provider's privacy controls.

11. Data Security

Aashu_Labs takes reasonable steps to safeguard information it handles, including support communications. Third-party providers use their own security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Bottle Colours features, advertising configuration, SDK usage, privacy practices, platform requirements, or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or support, contact Aashu_Labs at ashulabs.support@gmail.com.
